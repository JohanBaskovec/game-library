# Le Curseur: rules

## In short

Two teams, one phone. Each team takes turns making the other team find a position on a ruler between two
opposite ideas, using **a single word** as the clue. The closer the other team's cursor lands to the
secret target, the more points both teams earn. The goal is to score as many points as possible
**together**.

## Setting up

1. In the menu, enter the name of each team and choose the number of rounds.
2. Press **Play**. The team that starts is drawn at random.

## A turn

1. **"Next up: team X"** appears. Team X takes the phone and presses **Ready**.
2. Team X (the clue giver) sees:
   - two opposite ideas, one at each end of a ruler (for example *Peace* and *War*);
   - the secret target, drawn at random on the ruler.
3. If they do not know or do not like the ideas, they can ask for **other concepts**. The target does
   not move.
4. They type **one word** that, in their opinion, sits at that spot of the scale, and submit it. Passing
   is not possible.
5. **"Next up: team Y"** appears. The phone goes to team Y, who press **Ready**.
6. Team Y sees the two ideas and the clue, places the cursor where they think the target is, and confirms.
7. The result is shown: the target, the chosen position and the points earned.
8. Team Y now gives the clue: new concepts, new target, and so on until the last round.

## Points

| Distance between target and cursor | Points |
|---|---|
| 3.5 % or less | 4 |
| 7 % or less | 3 |
| 10.5 % or less | 2 |
| 14 % or less | 1 |
| more than 14 % | 0 |

The percentage is measured on the full length of the ruler. The cursor moves in steps of 1, so the
distances that actually occur are 3, 7, 10 and 14 or less.

## End of the game

After the last turn a recap lists every turn (concepts, clue, target, answer, points) and the total. The
score is shared by both teams, and the recap also shows what each team earned as guesser and as clue
giver.

One "round" in the menu means each team gives one clue: 5 rounds are 10 clues.
